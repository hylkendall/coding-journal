**正面**

You want an element to show the first keyframe during the delay AND stay on the last keyframe after the animation ends, so you write `animation-fill-mode: backwards forwards;`. What actually happens, and what should you write instead?
┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈
想让元素在延迟期间先套上第一帧、动画结束后停在最后一帧，于是写了 `animation-fill-mode: backwards forwards;`。实际会发生什么？应该怎么写？

**背面**

The value is invalid: `animation-fill-mode` takes one keyword, and two keywords separated by a space are not allowed. The browser silently ignores the whole declaration (no error), as if it were never written; unless another rule sets it, the fill mode stays at the default `none`. Write `animation-fill-mode: both;` (`both` = `backwards` + `forwards`).
Note: a comma-separated list (`backwards, forwards`) is a different thing: it gives each of several animations its own value.
┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈┈
这是无效值：`animation-fill-mode` 只收一个关键字，用空格隔开写两个不合法。浏览器会悄悄忽略整条声明（不报错），等于没写；没有别的规则设置它的话，填充模式就是默认的 `none`。应该写 `animation-fill-mode: both;`（`both` = `backwards` + `forwards`）。
注：用逗号隔开（`backwards, forwards`）是另一回事，那是给多个动画各配一个值。

**标签**

`css::animations`
